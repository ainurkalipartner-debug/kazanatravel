Three Days Through Almaty

A short, refined visit through Almaty's best — mountains, gorges, and the city's most loved tables. Three days, three landscapes, three dinners worth remembering.

Day-by-Day

D1

Shymbulak · Panfilov · Navat

Cable car to Shymbulak (the city in clouds). Afternoon at Panfilov Park & Zenkov Cathedral. Dinner at Navat — Almaty's iconic Central Asian restaurant.

D2

Almarasan · Medvezhiy · Gitsu

Morning hike at Almarasan Gorge. Lunch at Medvezhiy Resort. Evening dinner at Gitsu — slow, intimate, Georgian-Caucasian.

D3

Butakovka · Terrenkur · Gigi Mangal

Butakovka Gorge for the waterfall trail. Walk the Terrenkur Trail. Closing dinner at Gigi Mangal — open-fire grilled, locals' favorite.
