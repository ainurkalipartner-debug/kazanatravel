The Journey

Five days of curated Almaty — mountains, lakes and city, paced with care. Four nights of 4-star comfort in the heart of the city, followed by a magical night in the forest cottages of Oi Qaragai Resort.

Itinerary

1

Arrival · Almaty

Airport pickup. Check in to your 4★ hotel in central Almaty. SIM cards delivered. Welcome dinner at a curated local restaurant.

2

Kok Tobe · City Heights

Morning city tour. Afternoon cable-car up to Kok Tobe with panoramic views of the city and Tian Shan range.

3

Big Almaty Lake

Early departure to the turquoise alpine lake at 2,511 m. Hike Ayusay viewpoints. Picnic lunch at lakeside.

4

Oi Qaragai Resort

Drive to the eco-resort tucked in pine forests. Forest cottage check-in. Banya, dinner, stargazing.

5

Return to Almaty

Slow morning at the resort. Return to Almaty. Free evening — shopping, spa, or fine dining (we book).

6

Departure

Breakfast. Private transfer to the airport with farewell gift.
