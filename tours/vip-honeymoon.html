A Honeymoon, Designed

Five quiet nights between the soaring city skyline and the silence of the mountain pines. Swissôtel for refinement; Tenir Resort's cottages for the night you'll always remember — flowers, candles, the smell of pine through the window.

Itinerary

1

Arrival · Swissôtel Almaty

VIP airport meet & greet. Champagne welcome. Suite with mountain view at Swissôtel. Romantic dinner.

2

Almaty for Two

A slow morning. Boutique shopping. Afternoon spa. Sunset at Kok Tobe with a private table.

3

Big Almaty Lake

A private mountain day at the turquoise lake. Picnic by the water. Return for an Almaty dinner.

Tenir Resort — The Night

Drive to Tenir. Your cottage is dressed with flowers, candles, and a chilled bottle. Dinner privately served.

5

Return · Free Day

Slow morning. Return to Swissôtel. Last evening — your call: spa, dinner, theatre. We arrange.

6

Departure

Breakfast in bed. Private transfer with a farewell gift.
